## 1.4.5
- Make a plushie of any player you can see, wearing their current outfit (modded species, colours and patterns included).
- Name it, resize it, turn it, and raise or lower it with the Up/down slider, and move it with "Grab and move": click the button, slide the plushie across the ground with your mouse, click to drop it (Esc cancels).
- Nine poses: sit, stand, slump, hug me, wave, hands up, lie on back, lie on tummy and sleep.
- Walk up and press E to hug it: it squeaks, is pulled in to you, and is lifted to your chest if you are bigger than it.
- Keep up to 30 plushies, with 3 out at a time. Players who also have the mod see your plushies and can hug them too.
- Fixed very long colour names (like the rainbow pride colours) and accessories from mods with "+" in their name not showing on plushies placed by other players. Everyone who views plushies needs this version to see them.
- Deleting a saved plushie now asks "Sure?" first, so you cannot lose one by accident.

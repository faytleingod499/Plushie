# Plushie

Turn a player you see into a plushie. It wears the outfit they have on right now.

*This mod was created with the assistance of AI.*

- Click the bear button (or press **O**) and pick a player you can see. The plushie is put down in front of you.
- Give it a name, make it bigger or smaller, turn it to face any way, move it anywhere around where it stands (up to 5 metres each way, even down through the ground), and sit it in a pose: sit, stand, slump, hug me, wave, hands up, lie on its back or tummy, or sleep.
- Walk up to a plushie and a small "E  Hug" tag appears on it: press **E** to hug it. It squeaks, little hearts float up, and it is pulled in to you. If you are bigger than the plushie it is lifted up to your chest; if it is bigger it stays on the ground and you hug it round the middle. Walking away ends the hug.
- You keep up to 30 plushies (saved on your computer) and can have 3 out at a time.
- Players who also have the mod see your plushies and can hug them too. Without the mod, nobody else sees anything, and the mod does nothing to anyone else's game.

## Good to know

- Client side only. The only thing sent is a short line in the Steam lobby describing the plushies you have out.
- A plushie is a copy of the player's look at the moment you made it. It doesn't change if they change outfit.
- Put-out plushies are not kept between games: put them out again from the menu.
- Requires [GDWeave](https://thunderstore.io/c/webfishing/p/NotNet/GDWeave/).
